Add FitHerNova to your home screen for quick access — workouts, meals, gospel music and Coach Nova, all one tap away.
🍎

🤖
Android (Chrome) Tap the 3-dot menu → tap Add to Home Screen → tap Add.
💻
Desktop (Chrome / Edge) Click the install icon ⊕ in the address bar → click Install.
